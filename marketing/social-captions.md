# Social captions — AI advertising / consulting angle

For AiAppGenius. Posture: show the capability, let the cost gap do the talking,
no hard sell in the caption itself. Edit freely before posting.

TikTok `title` is capped at 150 characters — the short line is the one that fits.
YouTube Shorts descriptions have room for the longer version.

**Every post must carry the AI-generated declaration.** On TikTok that is the
`is_aigc` flag, which is non-optional for synthetic people. On YouTube it is the
"Altered or synthetic content" disclosure in the upload flow.

---

## 1. opal-walkout.mp4 — the transition ad

**Short (TikTok):**
Boutique to dinner table in 18 seconds. No crew, no studio, no model, no reshoot.
Built from a jeweller's own stock photos.

**Long (YouTube):**
A full jewellery advert with no photographer, no location fee and no model call.
The pieces are a real shop's actual inventory, lifted from their product
photography. The woman, the boutique and the candlelit room are generated.

What that replaces: a day rate, a studio, a stylist, and the week of back and
forth when a frame comes back wrong. What it costs instead: a few pounds of
compute and an afternoon of prompt work.

I build these for businesses that have good product and no advertising budget.

## 2. opal-candlelit.mp4 — candlelit set

**Short (TikTok):**
Every piece of jewellery in this is real stock. The room, the light and the
woman are not.

**Long (YouTube):**
Four pieces from a jeweller's live inventory, worn by someone who does not exist,
in a dining room that was never built.

The hard part is not making it pretty. It is making the product accurate — a
diamond that reflects candlelight instead of glowing like a bulb, an earring that
actually passes through the earlobe. Most AI ad work fails on exactly those
details, and the client is the one who spots them.

## 4. opal-theset.mp4 — product-led

**Short (TikTok):**
A jewellery ad where the jewellery is the subject. Face cropped out on purpose.

**Long (YouTube):**
Most AI fashion output is a portrait of a woman who happens to be wearing
jewellery — the eyes take the frame and the product is incidental.

This is the opposite: the frame runs from the lower lip down, or goes in on the
ear, the throat, the hand. The pieces are the largest thing in shot. For a
jeweller that is the difference between a nice picture and an advert.

## Notes before posting

- **Stagger them.** Five videos dropped in one hour on a cold account reads as
  spam to TikTok's own distribution. One a day is better reach for the same work.
- **These are client advertisements.** Posting them as portfolio is normal
  consultancy practice, but it is the client's product and the client's SKUs on
  display — worth a line to them first if the relationship is new.
- **Do not caption these as photographed.** Claiming a synthetic model is a real
  shoot is the one thing that would actually damage the pitch.
